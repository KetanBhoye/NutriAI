Privacy Policy

NutriAI · Last updated 17 August 2026

NutriAI is a calorie and nutrition tracker. This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, and how you delete it. It is written to be read, not to protect us.

The short version. We store the food, weight and activity you log so the app can show it back to you. We do not sell your data, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not use your health data for anything except the features you can see. You can delete your account and all of it from inside the app at any time.

Who we are

NutriAI is operated by the developer of the NutriAI app. For any privacy question, or to exercise the rights below, contact ketanbhoye11@gmail.com.

What we collect

DataWhy
Account — name, email, password To create your account and sign you in. Passwords are stored only as a salted hash; we never see them.
Food logs — what you ate, portions, calories, macros The core function of the app: showing your daily and weekly totals.
Health & fitness — weight, body measurements, steps, exercise, energy burned To calculate your targets, track progress and show trends.
Profile — height, age, sex, activity level, goals To calculate your calorie and macro targets. Sex and age materially change those numbers, which is the only reason they are asked for.
Meal photos, if you use photo logging Sent for analysis to estimate what the meal contains. See “AI features” below.
Device & diagnostic — app version, error messages To fix crashes. We do not use analytics or advertising SDKs, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Apple Health and Health Connect

If you grant permission, NutriAI reads steps, active energy, distance, exercise and weight from Apple Health (iOS) or Health Connect (Android) so your day’s totals are accurate without you typing them.

This data is read only when the app is open, is used only for the features described here, and is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, and never shared with data brokers. You can revoke access at any time in your device’s Health settings, and the app keeps working without it.

AI features

Some features — photo analysis, the coach, meal parsing, weekly insights — send the relevant part of your data to Google’s Vertex AI to generate a response. That means the text you type to the coach, the meal photo you choose to analyse, and the day or week of figures the feature needs.

Google processes this on our behalf as a service provider. Your data is not used to train Google’s models. If you never use these features, nothing is sent.

The shared food database

When nutrition information is looked up for a food, we save the result — the food’s name and its calories and macros — so the next person logging the same thing gets an instant answer.

A name you typed yourself is only added to this shared database once several unrelated people have logged the same name, which a personal label like “mum’s birthday cake” will not reach. The shared database contains food names and nutrition figures only. It never contains your identity, your portions, or when you ate.

Who we share data with

We do not sell your data. We share it only with the services required to run the app:

  • Railway — hosting and database.
  • Google Cloud (Vertex AI) — the AI features described above.
  • Google Sign-In — only if you choose to sign in with Google.

We may also disclose data if legally required to. There are no advertisers, data brokers or analytics companies in that list, and we do not intend to add any.

How long we keep it

Your data is kept while your account exists, because a tracker is only useful with its history. Delete your account and it is removed. Backups are retained for up to 30 days, after which deleted data is gone from those too.

Deleting your account

In the app, go to You → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and everything attached to it: food logs, weights, measurements, activity, goals and photos. It cannot be undone, and there is no waiting period.

You can also email ketanbhoye11@gmail.com and we will do it for you.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your data, and to object to certain processing. The app gives you access and deletion directly; for anything else, email us and we will respond within 30 days.

Security

Traffic is encrypted in transit with HTTPS. Passwords are salted and hashed. Access to the production database is restricted to the operator. No system is perfectly secure, and we will not claim otherwise — but we do not hold payment card details, which removes the most attractive target.

Children

NutriAI is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us data, email us and we will delete it.

Not medical advice

NutriAI estimates calories and macros and offers general guidance. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice. Talk to a qualified professional before making significant changes to your diet, particularly if you have a health condition or a history of disordered eating.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date above and notify you in the app before the change takes effect.
